Question 1: Part D: 
The Flesch-Kincaid score is not a valid nor reliable estimator as it 
relies only on the length of sentences and words. This can lead
to a paragraph with simpler words and sentences being graded
higher than that of a more complext text, even though it may
not be of similar quality. This implies that context of the text
itself is not relevant in the grading process.

Question 2: Part F:
By usinging the spaCy tokenization method, in coallition with removing 
punctuation and spaces, has resulted in relatively good f1 scores. I have
also been able to drop the max_features to 2500( a saving of ~17%). 
With this drop in max features, it is expected that the accuracy would
also drop, however, the accuracy was maintained from both original models.
In addition, the f1 scores for random forrest increased from 0.4097 to 0.4913.
In SVM, we see that the f1 results did take a slight decrease from 0.5628 to 0.5597.
With all of this in mind, I conclude that my custom tokeniser has worked very effectively.
